01 / The first game

Poli Fartride

A one thumb endless runner where cartoon politicians fly across the capital on nothing but their own catastrophic digestive decisions. Hold to fly. Release to fall. That is the entire control scheme.

02 / The rules

Three rules I do not break.

01

One thumb, no tutorial.

If a game needs a second hand or a screen explaining which button does what, it is not finished. You should be able to play it with the hand that is already holding the phone, standing up, on a bus.

02

Nothing to sign up for.

No account, no email, no cloud save you did not ask for. Your progress lives on your device. The games run on a plane, in a basement, with the wifi off, and the privacy policy is short enough to actually read.

03

Free to find out.

You get to learn whether a game is fun before anybody asks you for money. Ads are optional and buying anything is optional. If the first sixty seconds are not worth it, nothing after them will be either.
