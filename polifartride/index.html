01 / The whole game

One button. That is it.

There is no tutorial because there is nothing to teach. You have one input and you already know what it does.

Hold

Press anywhere and he lifts, powered by the one resource Washington has never run short of. Keep holding and he climbs.

Release

Let go and gravity takes over immediately. Threading a gap between a subpoena missile and a barricade is all in when you let go.

Regret

The longer you hold, the worse his face gets. Six stages, from smug and composed to purple, veined and venting steam. There is no seventh.
