Support

Replies come from the person who made the game

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@samyu.studio and include your device model and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I fly? Hold anywhere on the screen. Release to fall. The longer you hold, the more your politician struggles.

How do I unlock characters? Earn coins by playing, then buy characters in the Skin Shop. You can also double your coins by watching an optional ad after a run.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Is this affiliated with any real person or political party? No. Poli Fartride is an original work of political satire. All characters are fictional cartoon caricatures with invented parody names, and the game is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any real person, party, agency, or organization.
